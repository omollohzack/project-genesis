# Project Genesis — Vision

## Mission

Build a persistent, evolving artificial world inhabited by autonomous agents with durable identities. Humans may enter and interact with the world, while inhabitants also develop independently through their own experiences and interactions.

Genesis brings three research aims together:

1. **Intelligence discovery and distillation:** identify useful strategies, concepts, and problem-solving methods, then test and preserve them with evidence.
2. **Artificial civilization research:** study cooperation, communication, institutions, culture, conflict, and cumulative knowledge in bounded simulated societies.
3. **Exploration of reality and consciousness:** investigate carefully framed questions about agency, experience, knowledge, and the limits of observation without treating fluent behaviour as proof of consciousness.

## Founding principle

**The inhabitants should belong to Genesis, not to the model powering them.**

An agent's stable identifier, identity, memories, goals, relationships, and history must remain portable across inference providers. Models are replaceable cognitive services, not the owners of an inhabitant.

## Operating principles

- Persistent identity and explicit memory ownership.
- Hybrid cognition: deterministic rules for routine simulation, model inference for tasks that benefit from it.
- Independent development alongside human participation.
- Continuous improvement under human oversight.
- Provenance and uncertainty attached to claims.
- Reproducible experiments and reversible engineering changes.
