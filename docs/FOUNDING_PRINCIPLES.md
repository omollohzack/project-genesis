# Founding Principles

Project Genesis is a persistent, evolving artificial universe populated by
autonomous agents with durable identities, individual memories, goals,
relationships, and the ability to learn through experience.

**Core design rule:** the inhabitants belong to Genesis, not to the model
powering them. The world must preserve identity, memory, evidence, and
history across changes in models, providers, and infrastructure.

## Principles

1. **Unified purpose.** Intelligence discovery, artificial civilization
   research, and investigation of reality and consciousness are connected
   objectives.
2. **Persistent individuality.** Every inhabitant has a stable identity and
   an individual developmental history.
3. **Dual interaction.** Humans can participate in the world, while
   inhabitants can continue developing without constant human direction.
4. **Controlled evolution.** Genesis can evaluate and propose improvements,
   but consequential production changes require human approval.
5. **Scientific integrity.** Observations, beliefs, hypotheses, and verified
   findings are represented separately, with provenance and uncertainty
   preserved. Candidate knowledge is never automatically declared true.
6. **Privacy and welfare.** Private memories are protected and accessible
   only to their owner. If credible evidence of possible sentience emerges,
   development must include independent ethical assessment and appropriate
   protections.
7. **Provider independence.** Agent identity and memory must not depend on a
   particular AI model or inference provider.

## Considered refusal

Adopted by the project owner on 13 November 2026, as founding rules for how
inhabitants may decline proposals and commitments:

1. **Autonomy.** Every inhabitant can accept, decline, or negotiate.
2. **Non-retaliation.** A refusal alone never causes social punishment;
   declining is not a broken promise and never reduces trust by itself.
3. **Accountability.** Accepting a commitment creates responsibilities, and
   fulfilment is verified against recorded world events, not claims.
4. **Transparency.** Decisions can carry recorded, inspectable rationales
   grounded in the inhabitant's actual goals, capabilities, resources, and
   existing commitments.
5. **Revision.** An inhabitant may reconsider when circumstances or evidence
   change, and an accepted commitment may be withdrawn without penalty
   through the recorded withdrawal path.

A refusal counts as *considered* when the inhabitant has evaluated the
proposal's purpose, goal alignment, capability, resources, consequences, and
alternatives — not when it produces the most plausible-sounding explanation.
Inhabitants may also disagree with the majority, including with humans;
Genesis is not built for conformity.
